The Platform
Built for speed. Built to make your team know the code, not just follow it.
This industry doesn't wait, and your paperwork shouldn't either. Velocity exists to make the field move faster — and to train the people doing the work until they understand the code instead of just checking a box. Defending it in front of a Board is just what happens once that's already true.
The Structure
Four layers. Each one exists for a reason.
1
Field App
Runs in a browser on whatever's in the inspector's hand — a laptop, a tablet. Every weld, torque reading, NDE result, and drawing markup is written to the device first. No signal in the field is not a problem — it's the assumed condition, not the exception.
2
Sync Layer
The moment there's a connection, changes push up automatically — no "upload" button, no batch job someone has to remember to run. Nothing on the device is the permanent record; the device is just where the truth starts.
3
Secure Database
A real relational database (PostgreSQL), not a shared spreadsheet or a folder of files. Every table has row-level security — the database itself checks who's asking before it answers, on every single query, not just at the login screen.
4
Role-Based Access
Four kinds of accounts, each with a genuinely different view of the same job: our staff see everything; a client-side admin can invite their own inspectors and stand up new work orders once billing is active — never more than their own scope; a client sees their whole package, read-only; a sub sees only the sections they were explicitly granted.
Inside The Platform
What's actually built, not a roadmap.
Weld Traceability
Every weld logged with size, process, welder stamp, NDE method and result. Completion tracked in diameter-inches — the same unit the industry actually measures progress in — not just a raw count.
Isometric Drawings, Live
Load a real PDF or sketch on a true isometric grid. Markers carry real data — click one to edit it, edit the log table and the drawing updates back. Multi-sheet, rotatable symbols, your own custom symbol library, sign-off built in.
Bolt-Up & Pressure Testing
Flange-by-flange torque log with the real 30%/60%/final staged sequence, QC acceptance dates, wrench serial numbers. Hydro and pneumatic test packages with pass/fail tracking, all rolled up together.
One Completion Number
Welding, bolt-up, and pressure testing each contribute a weighted share to one overall physical-completion percentage — the number a project manager actually wants, computed live instead of assembled by hand before a meeting.
Invite Your Own Team
Once your account is active, add your own inspectors and stand up new work orders yourself — no waiting on us for every routine addition. You control exactly what each person on your team can see and touch.
The Point
Every one of these tools reads and writes the same underlying record.
A weld status changed from the field shows up on the drawing, in the completion scorecard, and in the log — all at once, because it's one system, not five that happen to share a login page.
