The Workflow
From the field to a defensible record — one path, five steps.
Built offline-first: the field never waits on signal, and nobody downstream is ever looking at a stale copy.
1
Field Capture
Welds, NDE, torque, tests — logged on site, fully offline. No signal, no problem.
2
Auto-Sync
The moment there's signal, it pushes to the secure cloud — nothing waits on a person.
3
Reviewed & Locked
Accepted results lock the moment they're filed. Every change after that is a logged, dated entry.
4
Access Granted
Each client, sub, or admin sees exactly the sections they were granted — automatically, nothing more.
5
Always Current
Weld logs, completion %, and dashboards update live — nobody has to ask "what's the status."
