WPS / PQR Programs
Every welding process. Every essential variable. Written to code.
Each WPS is written from its supporting PQR. The qualified ranges are worked out from the test record, and every essential, supplementary essential and nonessential variable is checked against the governing code before the procedure is issued. Then it goes into your company library, where every job picks from it and every welder's qualification is tracked against it.

Variables on every procedure
  • Base metals — P-No. and group, thickness and diameter ranges
  • Filler metals — F-No., A-No., classification, size, deposit thickness
  • Joint, position & progression — groove design, backing, positions qualified
  • Preheat, interpass & PWHT — temperatures, time at temperature
  • Gas & electrical — shielding and backing gas, current, polarity, heat input
  • Technique — string or weave, single or multi-pass, cleaning
  • Impact-tested work — supplementary essential variables when toughness is required
